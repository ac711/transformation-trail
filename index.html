Puzzle 1 · Beneath the dashboard

What does 84% complete really mean?

The latest Atlas pack reports Green. Delivery activity and spend look healthy, but the evidence required by service owners and the control function tells a different story.

Green

Delivery dashboard

84%
  • 12 of 14 milestones closed
  • Spend broadly on plan
  • Build activity almost complete

Outcome evidence

  • 2 of 4 domains use the new service view
  • One mandatory regulatory report is still running on the current platform
  • The efficiency benefit depends on retiring that platform

Handover evidence

  • Operational acceptance criteria incomplete
  • Final service owner not confirmed
  • Residual reporting risk still sits with the programme

Which fact most undermines the programme’s Green status?

Puzzle 3 · The portfolio collision

Four programmes, two scarce teams

Quarterly planning shows four programmes drawing on the same service-reporting specialists and release window. Only two can receive full capacity next quarter. The others need a controlled alternative.

Amber

Programme Sentinel

Creates a single evidence chain for customer-protection controls across digital onboarding and servicing.

  • Fixed regulatory submission date
  • Evidence test script agreed
  • Two local evidence gaps remain
If delayed: the submission date is missed and a remediation plan must be agreed.
Red

Programme Hyperion

Replaces an end-of-support privileged-access service used by critical banking platforms.

  • Vendor support ends next quarter
  • Privileged-access exposure remains
  • No safe extension beyond one quarter
If delayed: support cost and security exposure continue without a credible path to green.
Amber

Programme ClearLedger

Automates payment-reconciliation exceptions and strengthens evidence for year-end control testing.

  • Manual exceptions are growing
  • Automation is not yet production-ready
  • A manual control is viable for one quarter
If delayed: operations cost and accepted control risk continue, but a time-bound fallback exists.
Amber

Programme Atlas

Creates a common service, dependency and control-reporting view across four banking domains.

  • Common design is ready
  • Two domains are ready to adopt
  • Four-domain handover exceeds available capacity
If unchanged: Atlas crowds critical work. A two-domain first outcome remains viable.

Which next-quarter proposal best protects fixed obligations and growing exposure while still delivering a meaningful Atlas outcome?

Compare the quality and duration of each fallback, not only the headline urgency.
